## Scope

`components/Landing.tsx`, served at `/` to signed-out visitors. Visitor mode:
**Persuade**. Primary audience right now: competition judges, who arrive once,
skim, watch 21 seconds, and decide. Secondary: independent tutors.

Action: watch the demo, then **Start a lesson**. Proof carried on the page:
the video, the real notebook component, and the evaluation figures **always
stated with their sample size**.

Untouchable: product truth in PRODUCT.md, the `/` server gate and the
`?lesson=` invite path, and the brand commitment to two unalike surfaces.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the desk the lesson happens on, seen from above — not a
description of the product but the surface it runs on, with the demo lying on
it lit and the learner's page beside it. It refuses the category default of a
centred hero paragraph over a screenshot in a rounded card, and it refuses the
tidy three-column feature grid that follows it.

OWN-WORLD: The dark desk (`#241b16`) as a real lit surface at night, not a flat
background: deep shadow at the edges, a pool of light where the work is. Cream
ruled paper (`#fbf7ec`) as the one bright object, with its red margin rule
(`#d98a8a`) and punch holes. Ink (`#2f2a25`), the red strike (`#c0392b`), the
green correction (`#2e7d4f`), marker highlight (`#cde8d4`) and gaffer tape
(`#e7d7a4`) as the only other colour. Accent green (`#8fd9ac`) is reserved and
used once, as the lit key of the primary action. Type: Caveat at display scale
for anything handwritten, Inter for everything the machine says. No new fonts,
no gradients, no glass.

STORY: A judge understands in one screen that this listens to a real lesson and
writes the page a learner keeps; believes it because the page shows the actual
notebook component and states its measured numbers with n; and acts by pressing
play, then **Start a lesson**.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desk fills the frame, lit from upper left. Top-left, the mark
and wordmark, small. Below it the claim in Caveat at 4–5rem, set as ink on the
desk itself, reading as written rather than typeset. Beneath it one sentence of
Inter and a single lit primary action. Occupying the right half and bleeding
past the fold, the demo video as a screen lying on the desk — squared, inset in
its own panel, unmistakably an object on the surface rather than a card in a
grid. The cream sheet enters bottom-left, cropped by the viewport, promising the
page below.

FORM: The Desk — index 3 of my ordered list of seven structures (behind
lesson-as-scroll and the self-correcting page), dealt as the lead by seed
`c5ec2461`, scope surface, mode persuade. Locked by the user with no steer.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance
