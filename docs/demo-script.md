# Demo script

Two people, two devices, about three minutes.

## Before you start

- **Two devices.** A laptop for the tutor, a phone for the learner. Two tabs on
  one machine will not work: they cannot both open the camera.
- **Headphones on at least one device**, or sit in different rooms. Each device
  transcribes its own microphone, so without headphones the tutor's microphone
  also hears the learner and files their sentence under the tutor's name. A
  repeated sentence is dropped as an echo, but the attribution is still wrong.
- **Two accounts**, made in advance: one tutor, one learner. Set the learner's
  languages (Learning English, Speaks Spanish) so the glosses appear.
- **Do not open with "can you hear me".** The app deliberately ignores speech
  aimed at the equipment, so a microphone check writes nothing and looks broken.
  Test the audio before the lesson starts.

### Optional: two minutes of history first

The tutor's panel can only say "verb tense, 3 lessons running" if there are
earlier lessons. Before the demo, run two throwaway lessons: learner says one
wrong sentence, tutor corrects it, tutor presses **Lesson pack**. Thirty seconds
each, and the carried-over panel then has something real to show.

## The lesson

Stage directions in brackets. Lines are said aloud.

**[Tutor: Start lesson → Copy invite → send it. Learner opens the link.]**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Tutor** | Good morning Ana, good to see you. How has your week been? |
| **Learner** | Hello Mark. It was a busy week. **Yesterday I go to the office for a big meeting.** |
| **Tutor** | Yesterday I **went** to the office. When you use *yesterday*, the verb moves into the past, so *go* becomes *went*. |

**[Point at the notebook: the mistake struck through in red, the correction in
green, labelled "Verb tense". The tutor's explanation is underlined below it.]**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Learner** | Yesterday I went to the office. That tense is still difficult for me. |
| **Tutor** | It will come with practice. Tell me about the meeting. |
| **Learner** | My manager asked me to present the numbers. I was very nervous but **it finished good**. |
| **Tutor** | It **went well**. We use *well* for how something happened, and *good* for describing a thing. |
| **Learner** | Sometimes I cannot find the correct word and I just stop talking. |
| **Tutor** | There is a useful expression for that moment: **'to draw a blank'**. It means your mind goes empty and you cannot remember the thing you wanted. |

**[Point at New vocabulary: *to draw a blank — quedarse en blanco*. The Spanish
is generated; everything else on the page was said out loud.]**

**[Tutor: Whiteboard tab. Write:  go → went   eat → ate   send → sent
Then press Capture, and switch back to Notes.]**

**[The drawing is taped into the notebook as a Whiteboard capture. The learner
sees the same board on their phone while it is being drawn.]**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Learner** | I want to speak more confidently in meetings. |
| **Tutor** | That is a great goal. We will aim at that over the next few lessons. |
| **Learner** | **Last month I has three presentations and all of them was stressful.** |

**[Say nothing. Leave that one uncorrected.]**

**[Tutor: switch the header to Tutor. "Not yet corrected" is holding that exact
sentence, labelled Subject-verb agreement, with the carried-over weaknesses
underneath and one drill suggested.]**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Tutor** | Last month I **had** three presentations and all of them **were** stressful. |
| **Tutor** | We should keep working on past tenses before the next lesson. |

**[The pencil note resolves into a correction. Switch back to Learner.]**

**[Tutor: Lesson pack.]**

**[Show the summary, the corrected sentences, the flashcards with their Spanish,
and the gap-fill built from the learner's own sentence: "Yesterday I _____ to the
office." Then open All lessons to show the history and the trend.]**

## If something does not appear

- **A turn produced nothing.** Look at the label under it in the transcript:
  "heard, nothing to note" means it was correct English, "not lesson speech"
  means it was aimed at the app. Both are the app working.
- **The notebook stops filling.** Check the pill for "Reconnecting". Turn
  listening off and on again.
- **Every sentence appears twice.** The two devices can hear each other. Put on
  headphones.
